1. About the application

Monk-E Creator Intelligence is an internal business application being developed for authorized Monk-E personnel. It supports creator discovery and the review of creator and content information for influencer marketing and campaign operations.

Access is intended for authorized team members using approved business accounts and assets. The application is not intended to collect passwords for Instagram or Facebook.

2. Information processed

Depending on the connected Meta account, creator eligibility, permissions granted and features enabled, the application may process the following categories of information:

  • Business and authorization information: Page, business or account identifiers; names of authorized assets; permission and connection status; and access tokens or related authorization data needed to make approved API requests.
  • Creator profile information: creator identifiers, usernames or display names, profile URLs or images, biographies, categories, location or language information, and other profile fields made available by the relevant API.
  • Audience and performance insights: available follower counts, reach, views, interactions, engagement metrics and audience breakdowns.
  • Content information: post or content identifiers, links, captions, thumbnails, publication times, content types, and available likes, comments, shares or other content-level metrics.
  • Application records: selected creator records, metric snapshots, refresh timestamps, source identifiers, and operational logs needed to support the dashboard, scheduled refreshes, troubleshooting and auditability.
  • Deletion-request information: contact details and information supplied in a request so the team can verify and action it.

Not every field is available for every creator. Missing data is treated as unavailable, not as a zero value. The application does not intentionally request a creator's private login credentials.

3. How information is used

Information is used for legitimate internal business purposes, including:

  • finding and evaluating creators for potential brand collaborations;
  • reviewing creator profiles, audience insights and content performance;
  • supporting campaign planning, internal dashboards and reporting;
  • refreshing records, tracing data to its source, maintaining service reliability and investigating errors; and
  • responding to access, privacy or deletion requests.

Information is not intended to be sold or used by this application to build advertising profiles for unrelated third parties.

4. Meta platform data

The application can request information from Meta technologies, including Facebook and Instagram, using permissions granted to the app by an authorized user. The information available depends on Meta's APIs, the permissions approved for the app, the user's business access, the relevant creator's eligibility and Meta's own policies.

Meta independently operates its platforms and processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. The application does not bypass platform permissions or guarantee access to any specific creator's information.

More information is available in the Meta Privacy Policy and the Meta Platform Terms.

5. Sharing and service providers

Access to information is limited to authorized Monk-E personnel who need it for the purposes described above. Where the application is hosted or maintained using third-party infrastructure or support providers, relevant information may be processed by those providers only as needed to operate, secure or maintain the application and subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, to protect rights or security, or to respond to valid legal requests. The application is designed for internal business use rather than public access to creator records.

6. Security

Monk-E should restrict application access to authorized personnel, keep access tokens and app secrets out of browser code and public repositories, use HTTPS for hosted pages and API traffic, and apply appropriate access controls to stored records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; suspected unauthorized access should be reported promptly to the contact below.

For your safety: do not email passwords, access tokens, app secrets or other login credentials when contacting us.

7. Retention and deletion

Application records are retained only for as long as they are needed for creator research, campaign operations, reporting, security or legal obligations. Authorized administrators should remove information when it is no longer required. Some limited records may remain for a reasonable period in protected backups or where retention is required by law or platform obligations.

Any person may ask Monk-E to review or delete personal information about them that the application holds. Requests are assessed and handled in line with applicable law and Meta's terms. Verification may be needed to protect against deletion requests made by someone else.

See the Data Deletion Instructions page for the request process.

8. Your choices

Authorized users can discontinue use of the application and revoke permissions through the relevant Meta account or business settings. Revoking access may prevent further data refreshes; it does not necessarily erase records already held by the application, so submit a deletion request if you also want those records reviewed for removal.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application develops or its data practices change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. The published version should always match the application's actual operation.
