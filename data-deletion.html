Request data deletion

Use these instructions to request a review or deletion of personal information held by Monk-E Creator Intelligence.

How to submit a request

  1. Email tech@monk-e.in with the subject Monk-E Creator Intelligence — Data Deletion Request.
  2. Describe the information or profile you want reviewed for deletion. You may include a public profile URL or relevant account identifier to help locate the correct records.
  3. Do not include passwords, app secrets, access tokens, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information.
  4. The application team will acknowledge the request, ask for proportionate verification if necessary, and review the request against applicable legal and platform obligations.
What happens next?

If the information is held by Monk-E Creator Intelligence and can be deleted, the team will arrange its removal from active application records and confirm the outcome. Any exceptions, such as records that must be retained by law or kept temporarily in protected backups, will be handled under applicable requirements.

Revoking Meta access

If you authorized the application through Facebook or Instagram, you can also revoke its permissions in the relevant Meta account or business settings. Revoking access stops future authorized API access, but it may not automatically delete records already stored by the application. Send a deletion request as well if you want those records reviewed.
